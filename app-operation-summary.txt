Phase 1 — Splash & Auth
Step	Screen	Purpose
1	SplashScreen	Validates session on launch
2	OnBoardingScreen	3 onboarding pages → "Get Started"
3	profile.dart (ProfileFormPage)	Select account type (customer/broker)
4	phoneOTP.dart (PhoneOtpScreen)	OTP verification → lands on BottomNavBar



Phase 2 — Main App Shell
Step	Tab	Screen	Purpose
5	0	homeScreen.dart (HomeScreen)	Browse properties, filter, view videos
6	1	explorer.dart (ExplorePage)	Search/filter properties by region
7	2	userProfile.dart (ProfilePage)	Profile menu, bookings, payments, settings


Phase 3 — From Home Tab (Tab 0)
Step	Trigger	Destination Screen	Key APIs Hit
8	App-bar bell icon	notifications.dart	GET /notifications/get_notifications
9	Tap property card	brokerCollection.dart	GET /customer/broker-properties
10	Tap "Book" in collection	Payment sheet → itemDetails.dart	POST /payment/initiate_payment, POST /customer/bookings
11	Tap gallery image	itemDetails.dart → GalleryViewPage	GET /customer/properties/{id}/details
12	Tap video play button	TikTok fullscreen overlay	—




Phase 4 — From Explore Tab (Tab 1)
Step	Trigger	Destination Screen	Key APIs Hit
13	Tap property card	brokerCollection.dart	Same as step 9
14	Apply filters	Filter bottom sheet	GET /listings/search, GET /listings/nearby




Phase 5 — From Profile Tab (Tab 2)
Step	Trigger	Destination Screen	Key APIs Hit
15	"My Bookings"	bookings.dart	GET /bookings/get_bookings
16	"Payments"	payments.dart	GET /payment/get_transaction_records
17	"Notifications"	notifications.dart	Same as step 8
18	"Settings"	settings.dart	POST /gate-way/log-out-user
19	"Terms of Agreement"	terms.dart (TermsOfServicePage)	—
20	"Help Center"	help.dart	POST /users/save-user-info





Phase 6 — Deep Navigation Paths
Step	Path	Destination	Key APIs Hit
21	BrokerCollectionPage → Book → success	itemDetails.dart (PropertyDetailsPage)	Contact reveal flow
22	PropertyDetailsPage → back	BottomNavBar (pushReplacement)	—
23	Notification of type booking	bookings.dart	—
24	Notification of type payment/transaction	payments.dart	—
25	Notification of type propertyUpload/etc.	properties.dart	GET /gate-way/get-user-properties
26	Settings → "Terms"	terms.dart	—
27	Logout (ProfilePage)	OnBoardingScreen	POST /brokers/logout-user or POST /users/logout-user
